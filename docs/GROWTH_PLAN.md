# Growth Plan — From Order Execution to Scale

A phased plan to grow the organisation from a boutique couture house into a disciplined, scalable luxury-ethnic-wear business — using better production management, capacity, and data as the foundation.

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Months 1–3) | Build the Operating Discipline

**Goal:** predictable on-time delivery and clean data.

- Digitise every order in the Production & Merchandising app; retire Excel trackers.
- Standardise job cards, order sheets, and daily WIP updates.
- Baseline KPIs and publish weekly.
- Reduce rework via QC checkpoints before embroidery and before dispatch.

**KPI targets**
- On-time delivery: 60% → 85%
- QC rework: < 10%
- Order visibility: 100% tracked digitally

## Phase 1 — Capacity & Throughput (Months 3–9) | Grow Without Chaos

**Goal:** increase output per hub without quality loss.

- Capacity model per hub (Kolkata embroidery, Mumbai embroidery, cutting, stitching).
- Load balancing across hubs and empanelled third-party units.
- Sampling pipeline: 2-week sampling SLA for retailer approvals.
- Vendor consolidation: 2–3 trusted fabric vendors, negotiated fabric contracts.

**KPI targets**
- On-time delivery: 85% → 93%
- Throughput: +30% styles/month
- Sampling approval cycle: < 21 days

## Phase 2 — Product & Brand Lines (Months 9–18) | Recurring Revenue

**Goal:** move from one-off bridal to repeatable collections.

- Capsule collections per season (2/year) with pre-committed retailer buys.
- Reorder engine: track repeat styles per retailer; auto-suggest reorder quantities.
- Introduce 3 price tiers: bridal couture, occasion wear, ready-to-wear ethnic.

**KPI targets**
- Reorder rate: > 20% of styles
- Retailer retention: > 85%
- Non-bridal share of revenue: 30%+

## Phase 3 — Brand Retail & Direct (Months 18–36) | Own the Customer

**Goal:** reduce dependence on multi-brand retail partners.

- Flagship showroom + e-commerce for made-to-measure booking.
- Direct-to-consumer bridal appointment model (higher margin).
- Data-driven merchandising: best-seller fabrics, silhouettes, embroidery motifs by region.

**KPI targets**
- Direct channel share: 25%+ of revenue
- Average order value: +20%
- Inventory/stock accuracy: > 98%

## Phase 4 — Scale Infrastructure (Years 3+) | Regional + International

**Goal:** export-ready production unit with compliance.

- Move to a larger production floor with in-house embroidery capability.
- Certifications: ethical sourcing, compliance, export documentation.
- International bridal/occasion buyers; Dubai, UK, US ethnic markets.
- ERP-grade systemisation of costing, planning, and CRM.

**KPI targets**
- Export share: 15%+ of revenue
- Capacity: 2–3x of Phase 0
- Gross margin: +5 pts through sourcing & planning

## Growth Levers Summary

| Lever | Owner | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| On-time delivery discipline | Production Manager | P0 |
| QC rework reduction | Production + Master | P0 |
| Sampling SLA | Production + Creative | P1 |
| Hub capacity model | Production | P1 |
| Vendor consolidation | Sourcing + Accounts | P1 |
| Reorder engine | Merchandising | P2 |
| Direct retail channel | Leadership | P3 |

## How the App Supports Growth

- **Dashboard** — live KPIs for the P0 levers.
- **Production Tracker** — capacity and WIP data for Phases 1–2.
- **Reports** — weekly delivery risk, rework, capacity (Phases 1–3).
- **PO & style history** — reorder suggestions (Phase 2) and merchandising analytics (Phase 3).
